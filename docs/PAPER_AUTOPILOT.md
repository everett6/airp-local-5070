# Continuous paper workflow

Run Center → Start continuous paper workflow → confirm PAPER. Stop after current step requests cooperative shutdown; it can take until that step's time limit. Closing the app leaves the detached worker running. A PC restart interrupts it; click Start again to resume the saved queue. No boot service or timer is installed.

The frozen queue selects up to 1,000 distinct CIKs alphabetically from the latest cached membership year. Its manifest preserves company names and source hash. This is a coverage queue, not a historically unbiased universe or 1,000 positions. Completed and failed attempts stay recorded; interrupted companies resume. Research outputs live under backend/results/forward/deep_research; queue under paper_autopilot/queue.json.

Jan searches recent progress, financial outlook and risks using Yahoo and Google/Bing news, free article pages and SEC context. Eight tool rounds and a maximum 600-second research budget replace the short experiment profile. Two successfully fetched non-SEC article domains are required; inadequate coverage is a recorded failure, not a forced bullish decision. Site availability varies; multiple domains are not proof of independent reporting. Citation/number checks still apply. Bonsai judges the checked card; horizon allocations remain virtual proposals. Roles share one Jan model and are not independent researchers. No minimum waiting time is imposed.

The controller prioritizes existing paper order sync (every minute while idle), approved Jan/Bonsai filing decisions (every 15 minutes), and existing evidence-gated learning review (hourly), then researches the next company. Research can delay sync by the current step's duration. Existing scheduled jobs have a 30-minute reserved window and share the same lock. No lock or GPU is held during idle waiting. HALT prevents new filing decisions and company research; sync follows existing cancellation/reduction rules. Failures retain codes and logs; subsequent cycles may retry existing orders through their existing idempotent IDs. No automatic strategy promotion, mandate expansion, borrowed capital or registered trial execution occurs.

Engineering validation uses synthetic sources and simulated child processes. No profitability result or completed 1,000-company investigation is claimed. At ten minutes each, research alone could require about 167 GPU hours, excluding model loads and judgments.

## Shorter technology option

Run Center → Auto-trade 100 technology stocks starts a separate resumable queue, capped at 100 unique Information Technology companies from the cached latest-year membership file. The same frozen stocks become a persistent allowlist for new AI stock entries, including manual and scheduled runs. Existing exits and paired sector ETF hedges remain allowed. No model, package or universe downloads are needed. Some familiar internet businesses are classified in other sectors and are excluded. Stop the current continuous worker before switching research profiles; each queue retains progress, and switching does not silently disable the stock-entry restriction.

## Enforced entries, planning and latency update

The user authorized restricting AI stock trading to the frozen technology queue. Auto-trade 100 technology stocks activates `paper_autopilot/stock_policy.json` before any sync. The restriction persists after Stop, reboot, and switching research profiles; manual and scheduled `ai_picks.py` both read it. Invalid policy blocks all new AI stock entries while leaving exits available. Pending outside-universe simulator plans are skipped, owned outstanding entry orders are cancelled individually, and racing fills are refreshed. Exits wait for uncertain cancellations to resolve. Existing open positions retain their scheduled exits; sector ETF hedge legs for permitted stocks remain allowed. The frozen core ETF/crypto mandate is unchanged. These exceptions appear in the confirmation and planning page.

Four horizons now appear in deep Bonsai judgment: day 1, short 5, medium 21 and long 63 sessions. Directional ratings need checked source quotes; invalidation quotes must exist on the card. AI planning & cases uses completed evidence no older than 48 hours in the frozen queue, and presents balanced, defensive and long-focus virtual budgets. Failed/missing/PASS labels hold cash. One company receives at most 10% across horizons, no borrowing is permitted, and at least 10% remains cash. Selloff, flat, upside and liquidity-stress gains/losses use explicit hypothetical moves, a 20 bp per-side cost assumption (60 bp in liquidity stress), and one round trip at each horizon's own duration. Combined contributions are not returns over a common period or AI gain forecasts. No probability, winner, profit claim or deployment follows these simulations. The existing five-session sleeve remains the only executable AI stock strategy.

Latency changes: idle paper sync every 60 seconds, filing checks every 15 minutes, two-company batches when at least 45 minutes remain before the scheduled slot, and one company near deadlines. A batch loads Jan once and Bonsai once. Controller-only paper sync checkpoints reconcile between company research stages while retaining the owner's scheduler lock. Sync failures stop further checkpoint submissions; private GPU servers unload in finally. CPU sync needs six minutes of scheduled-job room; research still needs thirty. Research budgets are maxima, not forced delays. Sync can still wait for an active company stage, and entry-session/deadline/quote checks remain. Actual model-time gains have not been benchmarked on the GPU.
